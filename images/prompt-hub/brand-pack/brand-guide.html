Prompt Hub

One place for ready-made prompts. The brand is calm and exact, with one bright signal: a deep navy base, powder blue support and a single sunflower-yellow spark.

Logo

The mark is a hexagon hub: a prompt chevron and cursor at the centre, three nodes around it. The wordmark is plain type: "Hub" in yellow on navy, and navy with a yellow underline on light backgrounds.

  • Clear space around the logo: at least half the height of the mark on every side.
  • Smallest size: mark 16 px, full logo 120 px wide.
  • Use the navy-tile mark on light backgrounds and the lighter-tile mark on navy.
  • Keep "Hub" as plain type. Do not put it in a coloured pill or box.

Logo versions

Every version is in logos/ as SVG (scalable, for print and web) and PNG (transparent, 1600 px wide).

  • Use the full-colour logo on navy, white or light backgrounds. Use mono versions only for one-colour print, stamps and embossing.
  • On yellow use the "on-yellow" version: navy tile and navy text.
  • Use the stacked logo where the space is square or narrow; use the horizontal logo everywhere else.

Voice and rules

Do
  • Say what the prompt does in one short line.
  • Use yellow for one thing per screen.
  • Keep generous space; let navy breathe.
  • Name it "Prompt Hub" (two words, capital P and H).
Don't
  • Don't add gradients, glows or extra accent colours.
  • Don't stretch, rotate or recolour the mark.
  • Don't use yellow for body text or on white.
  • Don't write "PromptHub", "prompt hub" or "Prompt Vault".
