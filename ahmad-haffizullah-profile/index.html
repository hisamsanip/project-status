Profile

Twenty-two years of making buildings work

Ahmad Haffizullah started as an electrical engineer on Prasarana’s LRT stations. He has run buildings, portfolios and the teams behind them ever since.

His path runs through a university campus and a nationwide billboard network. At Glenmarie Asset Management (HICOM Facilities Management Berhad) he ran a 22-storey commercial office tower for its owners and tenants. He went on to look after eight hotels in Malaysia and Bali, then built a regional facilities function across Asia Pacific from the ground up.

At Urusharta Jamaah he helped manage a residential, commercial and investment portfolio valued at about RM1.1 billion. He coordinated asset enhancement and refurbishment projects there to raise asset value and long-term returns.

Today he heads Facility Management for Region 3 at PR1MA Facilities Management. He leads operations for 13 residential developments in Sabah, Sarawak and the Klang Valley, and carries each one from Vacant Possession through the Defects Liability Period to the formation of its Joint Management Body or Management Corporation.

He is a Certified Facilities Management Manager (CFMM Level 5) with CIDB Malaysia and a Professional Technologist registered with MBOT. He is recognised for leadership in crisis management, strategic asset planning and stakeholder engagement.
