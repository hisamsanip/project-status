How it's built

HyperFrames: title, chapter and end cards

Three GSAP compositions (intro, chapter, outro) rendered as transparent WebM and layered into the edit.

Remotion: the edit

Cuts, speed ramps, freezes, split screen, camera moves, script captions with word pop-in and highlight swipe, stickers and stamps, speed lines, flashes, letterbox, camcorder frame, grain and vignette. All timing comes from edl.json and cues.json.

Sound

Music bed (120 BPM, I–vi–IV–V) and every SFX are synthesised from code and timed to the same cue sheet: whooshes, pops, ding, shutter, impacts, riser and boom. The original location sound sits low underneath. The final mix is loudness-normalised to −14 LUFS.

B-roll

Cut from the supplied footage: the skate close-up crop, the crowd reaction cutaway, the polaroid freeze and the DAY 1 / TODAY split screen.
