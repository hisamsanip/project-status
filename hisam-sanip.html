Profile

A building is handed over once. It is run every day after.

I am a Facilities and Property Manager with extensive experience in the retail industry and in commercial and residential development, covering facilities planning, design, operation and management.

I started as a mechanical engineer installing and commissioning air-conditioning and chilled water plant. Since 2007 I have been on the other side of handover: running shopping malls, an office tower and, today, the facilities and property management of PR1MA's housing complexes.

My work now is mostly direction: setting strategy, designing operating models and building the team. I also develop my own AI-assisted systems to make operations more efficient.
